Book your Swell orders in Moneybird without typing them over. When an order is paid, it becomes a Moneybird sales invoice with its own invoice number, or an external sales invoice with the Swell order number, and its payment is registered so it shows as paid. Each refund becomes a credit invoice for the refunded amount.

Connect Moneybird with a personal API token, and choose whether new invoices are marked as sent, emailed to the customer by Moneybird, or left as drafts. Each order's Moneybird tab shows the invoice number, its status in Moneybird and a link to the invoice.

- **Sales or external invoices.** Sales invoices are numbered by Moneybird and use your workflow and document style. External sales invoices record the order with the Swell order number as reference.
- **Payments registered.** The order's payment is registered on its invoice, on the financial account you choose or as a payment without proof. Orders in other currencies are invoiced in their own currency.
- **Refunds as credit invoices.** Each full or partial refund becomes a credit invoice for the refunded amount, split over the order's tax percentages so the tax is reversed in proportion, and its refund payment is registered.
- **The right tax rate on every line.** Each item and the shipping get the Moneybird sales tax rate with the percentage your store charged, preferring a rate for the customer's country. Lines without a match get the default tax rate you choose, and the order notes them.
- **Customers as contacts.** Each customer becomes a Moneybird contact with their first order, with their name, address, email, phone and VAT number. Later changes to their account are sent to the contact.
- **Earlier orders too.** Send the paid orders of the last 7, 30 or 90 days in the background, or select up to 10 orders in the order list. Each step is saved on the order, so a retry continues where it stopped instead of starting over.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Moneybird, create a personal API token under Settings > Integrations > External applications, with access to sales invoices and settings. Paste it in the app settings, run Test connection to find your administration ID, enter it, and test again to list your sales tax rates and financial accounts. Then choose how orders are booked and place a test order.
