# Moneybird

Book your Swell orders in Moneybird automatically: invoices, payments and refunds, without typing them over.

## How it works

1. **Paid orders become invoices.** Each paid order becomes a Moneybird sales invoice with its own number, or an external sales invoice with the Swell order number. You choose in the settings.
2. **Payments registered.** The payment is registered on the invoice, so it shows as paid.
3. **Refunds as credit invoices.** Each refund, full or partial, is booked as a credit invoice for the refunded amount, with the tax reversed.
4. **Right tax rates.** Every line gets the Moneybird tax rate with the percentage Swell charged.
5. **Customers as contacts.** Customers become Moneybird contacts with their address and VAT number, and stay up to date.
6. **Send earlier orders.** Send paid orders from the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or pick orders in the order list.

## Setup

In Moneybird open Settings → External applications and AI connections → New API token. Paste the token in the app settings and run **Test connection** to find your administration ID and tax rates.
